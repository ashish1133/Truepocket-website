# Auth.md

TruePocket is an instant loan mobile application.
Our website (https://www.truepocket.live) serves as a public landing page for downloading our APK and viewing our features, terms, and privacy policies.

Currently, we do not expose any public APIs or OAuth/OIDC endpoints that require authentication.
Users must download the TruePocket Android application to authenticate and apply for loans.

Agent interactions on this website do not require authentication as all content is public.
